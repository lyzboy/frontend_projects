Business Name: Peak Prototyping & Fabrication

Location: Delta, Colorado

What They Do: Custom 3D printing (both FDM and resin), rapid prototyping, and 
small-batch mechanical part fabrication. They cater to local industrial clients 
needing replacement parts as well as hobbyists requiring high-detail custom 
models.

Business Description: A local, precision-focused manufacturing shop dedicated to 
turning digital drafting and CAD designs into physical realities. Peak 
Prototyping bridges the gap between digital modeling and physical manufacturing, 
specializing in durable mechanical components, custom jigs, and high-fidelity 
resin prototypes.

Employees: A tight-knit team of four:

1 Lead Mechanical Designer: Handles CAD reviews and custom drafting.

2 Fabrication Technicians: Manage the 3D printer farms (monitoring temperatures, 
swapping filaments/resins) and handle post-processing (washing, curing, and assembly).

1 Shop Manager: Handles client intake, material sourcing, and logistics.

Where They Operate: They operate out of a physical storefront and fabrication 
workshop on the Western Slope, serving local walk-in clients while also offering 
remote design and shipping services for regional customers.

Business Motto: "Precision Engineered. Locally Forged."

Logo Description & Colors: The logo features a geometric, low-poly mountain peak 
being actively extruded from a 3D printer nozzle.

Primary Color: Safety Orange (Hex: #FF5F15) - representing energy, 
fabrication, and industry.

Secondary Color: Industrial Steel Grey (Hex: #708090) - representing 
durability and mechanical precision.

Background: Designed to contrast well against Carbon Black (Hex: #121212) or 
crisp white.
